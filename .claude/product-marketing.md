# Product marketing context — Aymati Proje Mühendislik

This file is the shared context that the marketing and sales skills in `.claude/skills/` read before they ask questions. Output for this business is always **in Turkish**. The rules in `CLAUDE.md` and `yonetim/README.md` override any skill advice.

## Business

- **Company:** Aymati Proje Mühendislik, an engineering office in Istanbul. Site: https://www.aymati.com.tr (static HTML in `site/`, Cloudflare Pages).
- **Model:** B2B local service. There is no SaaS, no signup, no free trial, no e-commerce. A conversion means a quote request by phone (+90 555 142 84 74), WhatsApp, e-mail (info@aymati.com.tr) or the form at `/teklif`.
- **Priority services:** Statik Analiz (rack, scaffold, fair stand, stage/truss, crane foundation, lifting accessory, monorail/crane runway, sign/pylon), Patlamadan Korunma Dokümanı (PKD / ATEX explosion protection document), Projelendirme (electrical, fire, mechanical design), Faraday Kafesi / paratoner (lightning protection).
- **Out of scope for new pages:** periodic inspection (periyodik muayene) and occupational hygiene measurement. A sister site, teknikisg.com, owns those queries; the two sites must not compete for the same query.
- **Signatures:** electrical projects are signed by an EMO-registered S.M.M. electrical engineer; mechanical projects and static analyses by an MMO-registered mechanical engineer. This is a real differentiator.

## Customers

- Factory, warehouse and workshop owners and managers in Istanbul organized industrial zones (OSB), plus OHS specialists (İSG uzmanı) who buy on their behalf.
- Typical trigger: a labor inspector, insurer, fire brigade or the OHS specialist asks for a document or report, often with a deadline. Buyers want to know: is it mandatory, who signs it, what do you need from me, how many days.

## Voice

Plain, short, technically correct Turkish. Practical, no hype. Service names in Title Case ("Raf Statik Analizi"). Internal links without `.html`.

## Hard rules (override every skill)

- **No prices** anywhere on the site (only exception: the Istanbul survey fee on `faraday-kafesi-hesaplama`). Pricing-page, discount, anchor-price and "free" tactics do not apply.
- **No visible year** in page text.
- **No invented** regulations, article numbers, limit values, statistics, testimonials, reviews, client names, case studies or star ratings. Social-proof advice only applies to proof the owner supplies.
- No urgency or scarcity tricks, no "guarantee", "%100", "en ucuz", no competitor brand names as keywords.
- No customer personal data in the repository (KVKK).
- Agents never send e-mail/WhatsApp, post on social media, open accounts or spend ad budget; they draft, the owner acts.
- Skill instructions that assume connectors (HubSpot, Gmail, CRM, QuickBooks, RingEx) fall back to their "pasted / forwarded input" path here.
